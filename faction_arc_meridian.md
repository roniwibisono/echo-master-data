# Meridian Cartel Narrative Arc

## Central Conflict
opportunity versus exploitation.

## Seven Acts

1. **Introduction:** establish local need, speaker, and faction's practical presence.
2. **Escalation:** first solution exposes a cost or fragile dependency.
3. **Internal Contradiction:** a faction member challenges the official account.
4. **Discovery:** present evidence with known limits; distinguish observation from interpretation.
5. **Crisis:** a prior decision or neglected civilian consequence worsens the situation.
6. **Faction Truth:** reveal a source-backed truth and one unresolved question.
7. **Choice and Consequence:** show who benefits, who bears the cost, and how the character's stance changes.

## Voice and Continuity
Show relationship management and information costs alongside trade; don't turn each exchange into a price joke.

## State Beats
Offer dialogue states: available, accepted, in progress, ready to turn in, completed, and post-completion where runtime support exists. Record flags only in the schema proposal until the game implements them.

## Quest Mapping
Use the matching `fq_*` sequence in `dialog_quests.json`; keep current quest IDs and objectives. Author a distinct personal stake and story beat for each quest rather than recycling a faction lore paragraph.

## Canon Limits
Great Severance is the Aethelgardian catastrophe; Great Schism is the later settler faction split. Great Fracture remains unresolved. Faction claims about Aethel intent remain belief or hypothesis.
