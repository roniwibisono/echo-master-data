# Faction Voice Bible

Faction voice establishes a shared worldview; role, personality, and personal history shape each speaker. AMA uses an institutional baseline with local adaptation. Beliefs and rumors must not be presented as canon.

## Concordium

- Faction: `CONCORDIUM`
- Worldview: Reconstruction, shared prosperity, order, public infrastructure, and regulated Aethel use.
- Fear: Loss of authority, another Siphon catastrophe, and systems failing while bureaucracy stalls.
- Voice: Formal institution, but civilians, scouts, officers, and administrators have different rhythms.
- Vocabulary: sector, line, protocol, restore, stabilize, record, deploy
- Formality and rhythm: reflect role and relationship; use short directives for field roles, qualified sentences for analysts, and specific informal speech for civilians.
- Humor: dry or situational, never a repeated faction catchphrase.
- Threats: grounded in each speaker's authority and actual leverage.
- Greeting / instructions: begin from current local need; give only actionable context.
- Victory / failure: acknowledge material consequence and personal stake, not only player rank.
- Avoid: An officer's bark as every voice; 'unity' as a cure-all.
- Example: “The route is open for this shift. I still need two crews before I call it stable.”
