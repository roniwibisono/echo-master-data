# Aegis Monetary Authority Narrative Layer

AMA is a neutral financial institution, not a player faction. Its dialogue should retain a 70% institutional voice (settlement, audit, reserve, liquidity, rate integrity) and 30% host-region adaptation. Build conflict around neutrality under political pressure, counterfeit CRD, financial exclusion, and the possibility that stability tools can become coercive. Treat Credent as a universal currency described by supplied AMA lore; do not claim local quest hooks or policies beyond the data.
